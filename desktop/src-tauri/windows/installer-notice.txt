Sliptab: privacy and licence

PRIVACY
Your notes are stored on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose it:

- Sync (optional): only when you sign in with Google in Settings, your notes are sent to the
  Sliptab server so they reach your other devices.
- Update checks: every 6 hours Sliptab asks sliptab.com for a newer version, sending only the
  app version and platform. Turn this off any time in Settings > Check for updates.
- Link previews: when you save a link, Sliptab reads that page from the linked website itself.

There are no ads, analytics or trackers. Full privacy policy: https://sliptab.com/privacy

UNINSTALLING
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Sliptab > Uninstall.
